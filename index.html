Why this works differently

01

Agency aesthetics, commercial execution

I come from executive production and direction for branding agencies, and I work at the pace of the tech ecosystem. The result: pieces with narrative precision, a consistent visual code and a clear metric behind every creative decision. Beauty isn't the goal; it's the vehicle for conversion.

02

AI and automation applied to the process, not the result

Traditional studios run 100% manually. I design automated flows that compress the schedule without touching the quality bar:

  • Accelerated pre-production: ideation, outlines and scripting in days, not weeks.
  • Scalable post-production: systems that multiply the number of pieces while keeping the same finish.
  • Less dead time, lower logistics cost, less friction in every review.
AI doesn't replace direction. It removes what should never have eaten your budget.

03

One point of contact, start to finish

No coordinating between three vendors. No finger-pointing when something slips. Strategy, shooting, post and format adaptation under one creative direction and one accountable person.

How the process looks

Three stages, with one person accountable at every stage. Every project and every delivered piece includes 2 revision rounds.

Standard productions · 5 to 7 days

01

Pre-production

Planned with you before the camera rolls — timing depends on your revisions.

  • Content planning and adaptation with a high-impact, high-retention script structure.
  • Locations, shooting schedule and logistics.
  • Technical crew and drone coordination for key aerial shots.
Up to 3 days
02

Production

Shot in 4K, with the right support for the scale of the event.

  • 4K high-definition camera; requirements confirmed with you beforehand.
  • Camera assistant and lighting defined according to the scale of the event.
1 day · 5 to 7 hours of shooting
03

Post-production

Where the piece takes shape and gets ready to publish.

  • Professional editing and color correction.
  • Sound design and royalty-free music.
  • Final delivery in formats for social media or presentations.
≈ 2 to 3 days

AI productions · 3 to 4 days

A much simpler, automated flow. We work from your pre-recorded videos and generate digital avatars and high-quality, hyper-realistic B-roll shots tailored to each case: commercial product content or avatar-led UGC. No on-site shoot needed, so it works remotely — also for clients in other countries.
